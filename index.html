07 MEET THE TEAM

Two builders.
One shared frustration.

Voltify is built by two Gambian engineers who got tired of guessing where their electricity goes. We’re developing a low-cost smart energy meter on the ESP32 platform, designed for the realities of power use in West Africa.

Abubacarr “kTron” Keita

CO-FOUNDER

Abubacarr is a full-stack developer and embedded systems engineer from The Gambia, with a background in robotics and applied machine learning. He represented The Gambia at the FIRST Global Challenge in Singapore in 2023, and in 2026 he returned to the competition in South Korea as coach of the national team. He studies at the University of The Gambia, runs K-Tron, a local digital services business, and mentors high school students preparing for FIRST Global. At Voltify, he brings that hands-on hardware and firmware experience to turning raw energy readings into something people can understand and act on.

Cherno Basiru Jallow

CO-FOUNDER

Cherno is an AI and machine learning specialist and currently an AI intern at the United Nations. He is also a data science intern at the MRC Unit The Gambia at LSHTM and AI & ML Lead at OBENTaS Global. He speaks on AI, research and leadership, and is driven by building tech solutions for The Gambia. At Voltify, he leads the data and AI side of the platform, turning meter readings into insights people can use.
